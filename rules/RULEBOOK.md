# DECCAN

**2–8 players · 30–45 minutes**

Two armies form on one piece of ground. You do not command an army — you put units *into* one,
alongside whoever else wants the same ground, and the army that takes it pays out to whoever
actually won the fighting. Sometimes that is you. Sometimes you have just spent three good units
buying somebody else a victory.

Every unit you commit is gone for good, win or lose. Your faction is the whole of your war.

---

## Components

**Nine faction decks of twelve units.** Every unit shows a **TYPE** — HORSE, FOOT or GUNS — and a
printed **STRENGTH** from 1 to 9.

Every faction holds the same twelve numbers and four units of each type. What differs is which
type each number is printed on: the Marathas' nines are cavalry, the Siddis' are guns.

**The ground**, with three **FRONTS** — **Van**, **Centre** and **Rear** — and a row for each of
the two armies.

## Setup

Each player takes a faction and holds all twelve of its units. Choose a start player. Everyone
begins on nothing.

| Players | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Points to win** | 6 | 6 | 6 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 |

---

## A round

### 1 · Muster

Beginning with the start player and going clockwise, on your turn you **COMMIT** one unit face
down into an empty slot, or you **pass**. Turns keep going round the table until every player
passes in succession.

* There are only ever **two armies**, and each has one slot per front — **three units, and so at
  most three players**.
* The first player to commit to an army is its **LEADER**. To commit into an army **you are not
  already in**, offer the unit to its leader, who accepts or refuses **without looking at it**.
  Once you are in an army, you reinforce it freely.
* **A refusal does not end your turn.** Offer to the other army instead, or open it yourself.
  You may offer to each army once per turn. Refused by both, you pass.
* You cannot hold units in both armies.
* Passing is not folding. You may commit on a later turn.

### 2 · Charge

Turn every committed unit face up.

### 3 · Battle

Settle each front on its own.

> **HORSE rides down GUNS · GUNS break FOOT · FOOT holds HORSE**
>
> A countered unit loses its front **whatever its strength**. Otherwise the higher **STRENGTH**
> wins the front. On equal strength, nobody wins it. An empty slot loses to any unit.

The army that wins **more fronts** takes the **GROUND**.

Level on fronts, and the army that won the **Centre** takes the ground instead. If neither won
the Centre, nobody takes it.

If only one army was fielded, it takes the ground and wins every front it filled.

### 4 · Spoils

**Each player in the army that took the ground scores one point for every front they personally
won.** The beaten army scores nothing, and neither does anybody when no army takes the ground.

Then **every committed unit is SPENT** — laid face up in front of its owner, out of the game for
good. Win or lose. Pass the start player to the left.

---

## Winning

The first player to reach the target wins. If two reach it in the same round, they share it.

If a whole round passes with nothing committed, or nobody has a unit left to commit, the game
ends at once and the most points wins.

---

## Three things worth knowing

**One unit cannot take a contested ground.** One front is never a majority of three. A single
unit is an opening for others to join, a bid for the Centre, or a free point if nobody bothers to
contest you.

**Two units is a real army.** Win both fronts you contest and the ground is yours — two cards
spent for two points, where three cards buy three. Neither is right. Certainty costs cards.

**Everybody can see what your faction cannot do.** The Firangi have no cavalry and the table
knows it. What they cannot see is which unit you just put on the Van.

---

## Glossary

**ARMY** — one of the two sides forming on the ground. Three slots, one per front.

**COMMIT** — place a unit face down in an empty slot. It cannot be taken back.

**COUNTER** — HORSE beats GUNS, GUNS beat FOOT, FOOT beats HORSE. A countered unit loses its
front however strong it is.

**FACTION** — the twelve units you start with. When they are gone you are done committing.

**FRONT** — Van, Centre or Rear. Each is settled on its own.

**GROUND** — what the two armies are fighting over. Won by taking more fronts, or by taking the
Centre when the fronts are level.

**LEADER** — the first player to commit to an army. Accepts or refuses every unit offered to it,
without looking.

**SPENT** — committed, and therefore out of the game for good, whatever the result.

**STRENGTH** — the number printed on a unit. It never changes.
